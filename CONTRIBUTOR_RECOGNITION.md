# SystemFabric Contributor Recognition

SystemFabric is built through the work, ideas, testing, feedback, and generosity of people who care about open-source software.

We want to recognize contributions in a way that is meaningful, respectful, and transparent.

## 1. Who Can Be Recognized?

Contributions can take many forms, including:

* Source code and application development.
* Bug reports and reproducible test cases.
* Automated testing and quality assurance.
* Security feedback and responsible vulnerability reporting.
* Architecture discussions and research.
* Documentation, tutorials, and examples.
* Accessibility improvements.
* Community support and contributor onboarding.
* Design, usability, and developer-experience improvements.

A contribution does not have to be a merged pull request to have value.

## 2. How Recognition Works

With the contributor's permission, SystemFabric may recognize contributions through:

* GitHub pull request acknowledgements.
* Release notes and project updates.
* A public contributor recognition list.
* Community acknowledgements.
* Documented research or technical collaboration credits.

Recognition should accurately describe the work performed and its scope.

A suggestion, review, test contribution, or implementation should not be represented as something it was not.

## 3. Contributor Privacy and Preferences

Public recognition is voluntary.

Contributors may request recognition using their GitHub username, a preferred display name, or no public attribution.

We will not publish a contributor's private email address, legal name, personal information, or other identifying details without permission.

Contributors may request corrections or removal of their public recognition.

## 4. Security Contributions

Security researchers and reviewers deserve appropriate recognition for responsible work.

Potential vulnerabilities should be reported through the project's security disclosure process rather than publicly exposed before remediation.

Public acknowledgement of a security contribution will be made only with the contributor's permission and without disclosing sensitive vulnerability details.

Recognition does not imply that a reviewer has certified the entire platform or approved its production readiness.

## 5. Recognition Is Not Compensation

SystemFabric is currently an unfunded open-source project.

Recognition does not establish employment, a contractor relationship, payment, ownership, repository authority, maintainer status, or any financial benefit.

Any future compensation, sponsorship, or paid engagement must be separately agreed in writing.

## 6. Maintainer and Project Roles

Being recognized as a contributor does not automatically grant repository permissions or maintainer responsibilities.

Maintainer appointments and repository access are handled under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## 7. Licensing and Attribution

Contributions remain subject to the applicable project and third-party licensing terms.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [LICENSE](LICENSE), [COPYRIGHT](COPYRIGHT), and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

Recognition does not change copyright ownership or grant rights beyond those provided by the applicable licenses and agreements.

## 8. Growing Together

As the SystemFabric community grows, we hope to recognize the people who help improve its software, documentation, security, reliability, and research.

Our intention is simple: give credit honestly, respect contributor preferences, and make every meaningful contribution count.
